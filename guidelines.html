For content creators

Streaming & Video Guidelines

Last updated: 16 September 2026 · v1.0

These guidelines cover live streams, videos and other content that feature games developed or published by Crow Interactive. We want you to play our games freely and make whatever you like with them.

  • No permission needed
  • Monetisation allowed
  • Agencies & VTubers covered
  • Full game incl. ending
  • No copyright claims
01

What you can do

  • Live stream our games, and publish gameplay videos, Let’s Plays, reviews, guides, shorts and screenshots.
  • Any platform is fine: YouTube, Twitch, Niconico, TikTok, X, Bilibili, Kick and others.
  • Stream archives, highlights and clips are also fine, including clip channels run by third parties.
  • You do not need to ask for permission, and you do not need to let us know.
02

Monetisation

  • You can earn money from this content: platform ad revenue, channel memberships, Super Chat and other tipping systems, donations, sponsored content, and members-only or paid streams.
  • Registering videos for the Niconico Creator Support Program is also allowed.
  • We will not file copyright claims (including Content ID) against your content. If you ever receive a claim involving one of our games, contact us and we will help get it released.
03

Companies and agencies

  • Creators who belong to a company, talent agency or VTuber agency are covered by these guidelines. No separate corporate licence, contract or application is needed.
  • This permission covers every creator and channel under the agency, including the agency’s official channels.
04

Spoilers and scope

  • There are no restricted sections in our games. You may show the entire game, including the ending.
  • Demos, playtests and Early Access versions can be streamed too. The only exception is a build we have provided under a separate agreement (such as an NDA); in that case the agreement applies.
05

Music and assets

  • You may use the game music in your streams and videos together with gameplay footage. Distributing the music on its own, re-uploading it, or publishing it as standalone audio content is not covered.
  • You may use game logos, screenshots and key art (including press kit material) for thumbnails, stream overlays and channel artwork.
06

What we ask

  • Do not present your content as official Crow Interactive content, or suggest that we endorse or sponsor it.
  • Do not distribute game files or unauthorised copies of our games.
  • Follow the law and the rules of the platform you use. We may ask for content to be removed only if it is illegal or is clearly intended to harm others.
07

Credit

Not required, but we would be glad if you put the game’s name and its Steam store link in your description.

08

Covered games

All games developed or published by Crow Interactive, including future releases. Currently:

  • Corner Market
  • Fisher Idle
  • Game Store Simulator
  • Vending Machine Business Simulator
  • Homeless Life Simulator
09

Changes

  • We may update these guidelines. Content published before an update stays covered by the version that applied when it was published.
  • These guidelines are available in several languages. If the versions differ, the English version applies.
10

Contact

Questions about these guidelines: contact@crowinteractivegames.com
