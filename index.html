Crow Interactive is an independent game studio based in Turkey. We build compact simulation and idle games: corner shops, vending routes, fishing fleets, game stores. Systems you can read at a glance, and numbers you keep wanting to push a little higher.

After years of making mobile games, the studio moved its full focus to PC to work on deeper, more personal projects. Everything we ship lands on Steam, aimed at players who keep a second window open while they work, watch, or wind down.

Small team, short cycles, direct contact. If you have found a bug or want to cover one of our games, get in touch.

Content creator policy

All of our games are fully open for content creators. Stream them, record Let's Plays, and monetise your content freely. No permission needed, no strings attached.
